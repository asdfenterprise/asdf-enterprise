High Precision Weighing Controller
💬 Inquire on WhatsApp 📞 Call Sales

Microcontroller-Based Indicator Unit engineered for accurate and reliable load cell signal processing. Converts analog load cell signals into precise engineering values with a user-friendly interface for configuration, calibration, and parameter adjustment.

Technical Specifications

Display 6 Digits, 7 segments RED LED (0.56")
Keyboard 4 Keys Membrane (Index, Up, Side, Esc)
ADC Resolution 24-bit Delta-Sigma (16 Million counts)
Analog Output 4-20 mA, 0-20 mA, 0-10 V, 0-5 V (16 bit DAC)
Linearity Within 0.01% F.S.
Load Cell Input 4-wire technology, supports standard cells
Excitation 5 V DC at 200 Ω, max. 100 mA
Communication RS-485 (Isolated) / RS-232C, Modbus RTU
Baud Rate 1200 to 115200 baud
Digital I/O 2 Inputs (Remote), 2 Relays, 2 Transistors
Power Supply 10...28 V DC, max 250 mA
Dimensions 48 x 96 x 75 mm (Panel Mount)
