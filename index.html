High Precision Weighing Controller

Process Indicator ASDF-2601

Inquire on WhatsApp Call Sales

Microcontroller-based indicator engineered for accurate and reliable load cell signal processing. Converts analog load cell signals into precise engineering values with a user-friendly interface for configuration, calibration and parameter adjustment.

Technical Specifications

  • Display: 6 Digits, 7 segments RED LED (0.56")
  • Keyboard: 4 Keys Membrane
  • ADC: 24-bit Delta-Sigma (16 Million counts)
  • Analog Output: 4-20 mA, 0-20 mA, 0-10 V, 0-5 V
  • Linearity: Within 0.01% F.S.
  • Load Cell: 4-wire technology
  • Excitation: 5 V DC at 200 Ω, max. 100 mA
  • Communication: Isolated RS-485 / RS-232C, Modbus RTU
  • Baud Rate: 1200 to 115200 baud
  • Digital I/O: 2 Inputs, 2 Relays, 2 Transistors
  • Power: 10...28 V DC, max 250 mA
  • Dimensions: 48 x 96 x 75 mm, panel mount
